# Grad To Gig Connect

Students often face difficulties in finding relevant job opportunities and tracking recruitment activities conducted by different organizations. Placement departments also require an efficient mechanism to manage job postings, student applications, recruitment schedules, and selection results. Implement a system that aims to provide a centralized campus recruitment portal connecting students, recruiters, and placement administrators. It will facilitate job posting, application management, eligibility verification, interview scheduling, and selection tracking.
